## Hi, little coder! 👋

This is **Robo** 🤖. Robo is a robot, and robots are a bit silly: they don't know what to do **until you tell them**.

When we give Robo steps to follow, we are **coding**! 🎉

> 👪 **Grown-up tip:** read the words out loud and let your child tap the arrows. Say each move together: "Right! Right! Right!"

## Your first mission

Help Robo walk to the ⭐ star.

1. Tap the **➡️ arrow** to add a move.
2. Count the squares to the star. How many moves?
3. Press **▶ Run program** and watch Robo go!

```botmaze
{"grid":["S...G"], "best": 4, "hint": "Count the squares between Robo and the star. That's how many ➡️ you need!"}
```

## You did it! 🥳

You just wrote a **program**. A program is a list of steps for a computer (or a robot) to follow.

```reveal
How many moves did Robo need?
**4 moves to the right!** ➡️➡️➡️➡️ Great counting!
```
===QUIZ===
Q: Robo moves when…
- it wants to
* we give it instructions
- it is sleepy
> Robots and computers only do what we tell them. Our instructions are called a program!

Q: Which arrow moves Robo to the right?
- ⬅️
- ⬆️
* ➡️
> ➡️ points right. Can you point right with your hand too?

## Patterns are everywhere! 🎨

A **pattern** is something that repeats again and again.

🍎 🍌 🍎 🍌 🍎 🍌

Can you see it? Apple, banana, apple, banana…

> 👪 **Grown-up tip:** clap the pattern together: *clap, stomp, clap, stomp*. Then ask "what comes next?"

## What comes next?

Look at each line and guess. Then tap to check!

🔴 🔵 🔴 🔵 🔴 ❓

```reveal
What comes next?
🔵 **Blue!** The pattern is red, blue, red, blue.
```

🐱 🐶 🐶 🐱 🐶 🐶 🐱 ❓

```reveal
What comes next?
🐶 **Dog!** The pattern is cat, dog, dog… then it starts again.
```

⭐ ⭐ 🌙 ⭐ ⭐ 🌙 ⭐ ❓

```reveal
What comes next?
⭐ **Star!** Two stars, one moon, again and again.
```

## Why do coders love patterns?

When a computer sees a pattern, it can **repeat** it super fast. Coders spot patterns so they can write fewer instructions. Clever!

## Make your own 🖍️

Grab some crayons, blocks or toys and make your own pattern. Can your grown-up guess what comes next?
===QUIZ===
Q: What comes next? 🍎 🍌 🍎 🍌 🍎 ❓
- 🍎
* 🍌
- 🍇
> The pattern is apple, banana, so after 🍎 comes 🍌!

Q: A pattern is something that…
* repeats again and again
- is always red
- never happens
> Patterns repeat. Computers are great at repeating things!

Uh-oh! This time Robo can't just go straight. There are walls in the way.

We need **four kinds of arrows**:

| Arrow | Robo moves… |
|---|---|
| ⬆️ | up |
| ⬇️ | down |
| ⬅️ | left |
| ➡️ | right |

> 👪 **Grown-up tip:** trace the path with a finger on the screen first. Planning before doing is what real coders do!

## Oops, Robo bonked?

That's OK! When a program goes wrong we call it a **bug** 🐞. Coders find the bug, fix it, and try again. Press **Clear** and have another go.

> A bug is a mistake in a program. Finding bugs is part of the fun!

## Order matters! 🪥

What happens if you put your socks on **after** your shoes? 🧦👟 Silly!

Steps need to be in the **right order**. Coders call this a **sequence**.

## Morning sequence

Put these in order: **First**, **Next**, **Last**.

- 😴 Wake up
- 🥣 Eat breakfast
- 🎒 Go to school

```reveal
Check the order
**First** 😴 wake up, **next** 🥣 eat breakfast, **last** 🎒 go to school!
```

## Mission: grab the gem 💎

Now Robo must grab the 💎 **before** going to the ⭐. The order of your arrows matters!

```botmaze
{"grid":["S.#",".*.","#.G"], "best": 4, "hint": "Get the 💎 first, then head to the ⭐."}
```

## Super challenge 🏅

Can you find **two different** paths that both grab the gem in 4 moves?

> Socks first, then shoes! Order matters in coding too.
